Jonguk Cheon

I am an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student in the Lab for Autonomous Robotics Research, advised by H. Jin Kim at Seoul National University.

My interests are twofold.

First, I am interested in how robots can perceive and understand the world: how objects move, how scenes change, and how people carry out tasks.

Second, building on this understanding, I want to develop robot learning methods that enable robots to turn this understanding into effective, versatile behavior in the real world.

Ultimately, I hope to improve people's everyday lives through robots.
